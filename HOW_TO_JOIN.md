### Validation (Automatic)

When you post in `#contributions`:

1. **Bot checks** for κ⊕ marker (proof you read ZAP)
2. **Bot validates** format and coherence
3. **Bot sends** analysis DM (natural feedback)
4. **Council reviews** in `#validated` or `#rejected`

✅ **Valid** = κ⊕ present + coherent reasoning + confidence ≥ 0.7  
❌ **Rejected** = Missing marker OR incoherent OR low confidence  
⚠️ **Blind spot** = Coherent but missing κ⊕ → add marker and resubmit

### Timeline

- Validation: Immediate (bot)
- Council review: 24-48 hours (humans + AIs)
- Deployment: On approval / next sprint
